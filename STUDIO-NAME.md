# Studio Name: Cleave Studios

## Why "Cleave"

"Cleave" is an auto-antonym — it means both *to split apart* and *to cling tightly to*. That dual reading runs through all five titles in our line-up:

- **Ember & Echo** — emotions splitting from and clinging to the self.
- **Constellate** — discrete stars holding together as a single shape.
- **Chorus** — separate notes binding into a chord.
- **Grafted** — defeated foes severed and joined into new beasts.
- **The Lasting** — moments cleaved from the loop, and the loop holding fast.

One word, two readings, every game covered. "Studios" was chosen over "Games" to differentiate from the Cleave Gaming Dota 2 esports team and to read as a development house rather than a publisher of cleave-mechanic content.

## Namespace check (April 2026)

No competing studio with the exact name "Cleave Studios" was found on Steam, itch.io, the USPTO Trademark Search, or in indie-studio directories.

### Adjacent risks to monitor

- **Cleave Gaming** — Russian Dota 2 esports team. Different goods/services, so not a trademark blocker, but phonetically close. The "Studios" suffix mitigates direct confusion.
- **Game titles named *Cleave*** — Nintai Studios' boomer shooter (Steam app 3367620), GrizzlyBee Games' *Cleave Together*, Dark Cocooning's *Goblin Dice and Cleave*. These are title collisions, not studio collisions. SEO will surface them before us.
- **Combat-mechanic noise** — "cleave" is a common ability term in WoW, Vermintide, Heroes of Newerth, Epic Seven, etc. Search noise is significant but does not block use.
