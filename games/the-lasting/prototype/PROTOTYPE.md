# The Lasting — Paper Prototype Brief

*Phase 2. No code. Goal: in 5 minutes of play, decide whether the
"knowledge persists, items reset" loop mechanic produces a
satisfying second-loop "I know what to do" feeling.*

## Hypothesis

Replaying the **same combat scenario** with the same companion
roster but with **knowledge from loop 1** carried by the player
produces a meaningfully different outcome and decision tree in
loop 2 — and that difference is felt by turn 3 of loop 2, not
loop 1.

The Lasting is the only prototype that **must run two combats
back-to-back** to test its core thesis. Plan for this in the
session.

## Materials

- 12 **companion cards** — 4 Fatalist, 4 Defiant, 4 Schemer, each
  with: name, archetype label, one **signature ability** sentence,
  HP (4), base d6 attack.
- 3 **enemy cards** (the catastrophe's first heralds — fixed deck,
  same in both loops).
- 6 d6 dice, HP tokens.
- 6 blank index cards labeled **"Knowledge"** — the player writes
  notes here between loops; these persist.
- A timer or stopwatch (visible to the player).

## Setup

### Loop 1 (target 2 minutes)

1. Player drafts a party of **3 companions** from the 12.
2. Combat begins against the same fixed 3-enemy deck.
3. Combat is **ATB-by-initiative**: each round, every unit rolls
   1d6; resolve actions lowest-to-highest.
4. Each turn, an active companion can: attack (1 die, 4+ deals 1
   damage), or use their signature ability (1× per battle, with the
   effect printed on the card).
5. Battle ends at wipe or 5-turn timeout.

**Crucially**: between turns, the player can write up to 2
sentences on a Knowledge card — observations about enemy patterns,
which abilities synergized, what *almost* worked.

### Reset (10 seconds)

1. Reset all HP, all signature-ability cooldowns, all positions.
2. Player keeps **all Knowledge cards** they wrote.
3. Player **may swap up to 1 companion** in their party (simulating
   knowing-where-to-find a new companion in a re-recruit).

### Loop 2 (target 2 minutes)

1. Same enemy deck, same combat rules.
2. Player can reference Knowledge cards freely.
3. Battle ends at wipe or 5-turn timeout.

## Success criteria

The prototype passes if:

- **Loop 2 plays observably differently** by turn 3, driven by the
  Knowledge cards (not just by dice variance). Typical signals:
  - The player uses a signature ability in a different turn order.
  - The player swapped one companion based on what they learned.
  - The player targets a specific enemy first, with a stated
    reason rooted in loop 1.
- **The "I know what to do" feeling** is reported by the player
  unprompted when asked "how did loop 2 feel?"
- **Knowledge cards** were used in loop 2 — not just written and
  ignored.

If loop 2 plays identically to loop 1, or the player ignores their
Knowledge cards, the persistence-design is not yet pulling its
weight.

## What to log

- Loop 1 outcome (win / loss / timeout) and turn count.
- Loop 2 outcome and turn count.
- Number of Knowledge cards written.
- Number of Knowledge cards consulted in loop 2.
- Whether the player swapped a companion at reset.
- One sentence on whether loop 2 felt **smarter** or **the same**.
- One sentence on the player's emotional reaction to the reset
  itself (relief? frustration? curiosity?).

## Constraints

- No items. No equipment. No 30-day calendar in this prototype —
  only a single combat scenario, doubled.
- No story vignette, no kingdom, no catastrophe reveal. Combat-loop
  test only.
- No birth-sign mechanical effects beyond the printed signature
  ability.
- Two loops only. Don't run a loop 3 in this session.
