# Constellate — Star-Forged Companions

*Zodiac flagship. Phase 0 — `DESIGN.md` lands in Phase 1.*

> A world fragmented into floating sky-isles, each under a different
> celestial sign. **Asterix** creatures are born when starlight imprints
> on living matter. You are a **Stargazer** who maps constellations to
> summon and bond them.

## Quick spec

- **Genre**: ATB-style 4v4 party JRPG with zodiac synergies
- **Verb**: summon
- **Cosmology**: three tiers — the Greater Constellations, the Birth
  Signs, the Beast Court (see `docs/IP-GUIDELINES.md` for zodiac
  handling rules)
- **Identity**: zodiac flagship; full celestial cosmology
