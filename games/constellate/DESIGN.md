# Constellate — Star-Forged Companions

*Zodiac flagship. v0 design doc — one-page brief per the Phase 1 spec.*

## 3-sentence pitch (verification gate)

You play the heir to a fallen Stargazer line, reclaiming the craft of
charting celestial signs to summon and bond **Asterix** — creatures
born when starlight imprints on living matter. Combat is ATB-style
4v4 where adjacent party slots resonate by **zodiac synergy**, and
the sky alignment shifts as the campaign advances, rotating which
signs are dominant. It's a high-myth political JRPG built on a fully
owned three-tier cosmology of Greater Constellations, Birth Signs,
and a twelve-house Beast Court whose allegiances re-route every
chapter.

## Hook

The world fragmented long ago into floating sky-isles, each held
aloft by the celestial sign that hangs above it. Asterix emerge when
starlight settles on living matter; **Stargazers** call them down by
charting the sky. Your line read the sky better than anyone — then it
fell. You inherit what's left: half a star-chart, one living Asterix,
and a dispossessed name. Reclaim the craft, and the sky reveals what
was taken from you.

## Core loop

1. **Sky-mapping** — between battles, chart unfamiliar constellations
   on the overworld; mapping unlocks summoning rights and reveals
   sky-isles that were hidden to you.
2. **Tactical battle** — 4v4 ATB. Slot adjacency creates **zodiac
   synergy** buffs (e.g., Birth-Sign-aligned Asterix flanking a
   Greater-Constellation family member) or clashes (rival Beast
   Court allegiances, opposing celestial signs).
3. **Beast Court politics** — at each chapter pivot, choose which
   Beast Court house to align with. Reputation accumulates across
   houses; switching later costs narrative and unlocks alternate
   branches.
4. **Asterix recruitment** — bind through sky-mapping rituals tied
   to the rising Greater Constellation; family members of the same
   constellation evolve together.
5. **Sky alignment progression** — in-game time advances with story
   beats, rotating which signs are dominant. (Mobile port adds an
   optional **"real-sky" cosmetic mode** that ties alignment to
   real-world time — opt-in only, no exclusive content gated behind
   it.)

## Three-tier celestial cosmology

The IP heart of the game. Three discrete tiers, each doing distinct
mechanical work:

- **The Greater Constellations** (12+ originals) — creature
  *families*. Each constellation defines 3–5 Asterix that share
  visual lineage and a core ability theme. This is the "type chart"
  of the game; matchup knowledge lives here.
- **The Birth Signs** (12 originals, Western-zodiac-inspired but
  renamed) — the player picks one at character creation. Determines
  starter Asterix + an innate ability (FF job-class equivalent: a
  passive bonus and a signature skill that shapes the build).
- **The Beast Court** (12 originals, Chinese-zodiac-inspired but
  renamed and redesigned) — faction leaders ruling regions of the
  world. Per-chapter allegiance choices affect which exclusive
  Asterix you can recruit, which story branches open, and which
  sky-isles welcome you.

Names and silhouettes for all 36 are reserved for `LORE.md` and
`CREATURES.md`; per `docs/IP-GUIDELINES.md`, archetypes only
*gesture* at known zodiacs (e.g., a horned charging beast in place
of Aries).

## Original IP

Stargazers, Asterix, the **summon**-by-charting verb, and the
three-tier celestial cosmology are original to Cleave Studios. The
zodiac references are deliberately layered — Western (Birth Signs),
Chinese (Beast Court), and original (Greater Constellations) — so no
single referenced system dominates the IP. See `docs/IP-GUIDELINES.md`
for the originality bar and the naming-collision check process.

## Episodic plan

Each chapter = one new sky-isle + one rising Greater Constellation +
one Beast Court house in regional play.

| Chapter | Teaches | Beast Court state |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ATB + adjacency synergy | First house introduced; tutorial allegiance |
| 2 | Beast Court politics | First binding allegiance choice with real consequences |
| 3 | Greater Constellation families | Second house — rival to chapter 2's |
| 4–6 | Political escalation across houses | Multiple houses in conflict; reputation tradeoffs |
| 7+ | Cosmological reveal — what fell with the player's line | Endgame house realignment |

Paid chapter expansions can introduce additional Greater
Constellations and unlock Beast Court houses not seen in the base
campaign.

## V-slice scope (Phase 3 target)

Per the original plan: **1 hub, 1 vignette, 1 battle, 4 starters,
1 evolution event. ~30 min of polished play.**

- **Hub**: the tutorial sky-isle — the player's ancestral ruins. One
  walkable courtyard, one mentor figure, one star-chart fragment to
  read.
- **Vignette**: discovering the half-chart; a Beast Court envoy
  arrives offering allegiance. The player declines, accepts, or
  hedges — biases the V-slice's combat composition.
- **Battle**: the first summoning-ritual battle. 4v4 ATB. Demonstrates
  Birth-Sign innate ability + one Greater Constellation family +
  adjacency synergy on a single slot pair.
- **Starters**: four Asterix drawn from four distinct Birth Signs —
  selected so each Birth Sign choice in character creation lands the
  player on a *different* starter, exposing the same V-slice through
  four different opening hands.
- **Evolution event**: when the Greater Constellation rises mid-
  battle, one Asterix evolves — a one-shot tutorial of the family-
  level evolution mechanic.

Verification: ≥40% itch.io demo completion + ≥60% "would buy" signal
on the V-slice (per `references/original-plan.md` Verification gates).

## Tone

*FF XII / FF Tactics*-grade high-myth grand. Political weight,
mature register, restraint. Sky as cathedral, not as anime spectacle.
Music leans orchestral with sparse vocal lines.

## Strategic role

Studio flagship. Largest scope of the slate, IP-richest, and the
testbed for handling zodiac-themed content in a fully-owned way. Per
the original plan, this is the strong alternate to Ember & Echo if
the paper-prototype phase shows zodiac cosmology excites players
more than memory-bound tactics.

## Open questions for paper-prototype phase

- Does ATB 4v4 with adjacency synergy stay legible without becoming
  spreadsheet-y? Where's the legibility ceiling for synergy rules?
- How many Asterix should be available at any time without
  overwhelming the player's planning load?
- Sky alignment cadence — how many chapters per dominant-sign shift?
- Does the optional real-sky cosmetic mode cannibalize Steam audience
  attention, or does it cleanly stay scoped to mobile?
- Beast Court allegiance — how many chapters before a switch becomes
  punitive enough to feel meaningful but not so punitive it locks
  players in?
