# Cleave Studios — IP Guidelines

Studio-wide rules every project must follow. Designed to keep our
output **fully owned, defensibly original, and free of patent
collisions** with major creature-collector franchises.

## Originality bar

Every project ships with:

- **Original world names.**
- **Original creature/character taxonomy** (no Pokémon-tier elemental
  type charts; no Digimon-tier evolution stages by name; no
  MTG-tier color identities).
- **Original visual silhouettes.** Where mythologies are referenced
  (zodiacs, Western/Chinese folklore), reference but do not
  appropriate. Names and silhouettes must be original; archetypes may
  *gesture* at known imagery (e.g., a horned charging beast in place
  of Aries) but must not reproduce it.

## Patent-avoidance verbs

To stay clear of Nintendo's recent creature-catching patents, the
studio uses these verbs across the slate. **No throw / capture /
mount sequence** in any game.

| Game | Verb | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Ember & Echo | **bind** | Reverists bind Echoes formed from memory. |
| Constellate | **summon** | Stargazers summon Asterix by mapping constellations. |
| Chorus | **compose** | Positioning on the grid composes harmonies; magic emerges from composition. |
| Grafted | **build / graft** | Players build creatures from harvested essences. Explicitly *not* catching. |
| The Lasting | **remember** | Companions are recruited via knowledge accumulated across loops. |

## Zodiac handling

Three zodiac systems are referenced across the slate, but only
**one game leans on them mechanically**.

- **Constellate (flagship)** — full three-tier celestial cosmology:
  - **The Greater Constellations** (12+ original constellations) →
    creature *families* with their own mythology.
  - **The Birth Signs** (12 originals, Western-zodiac-inspired but
    renamed) → starter Asterix + innate ability.
  - **The Beast Court** (12 originals, Chinese-zodiac-inspired but
    renamed and redesigned) → faction leaders + region politics.
- **The Lasting** — light flavor only: 12 birth-sign archetypes
  inform companion personalities, support conversations, story
  branches, and one signature ability each. Narrative-flavored, not
  system-defining.
- **Other three games** (Ember & Echo, Chorus, Grafted) — no zodiac.

This separation keeps Constellate distinctive and prevents the slate
from feeling thematically homogeneous.

## Naming-collision check (process)

Before any new IP name (game title, in-fiction proper noun, faction,
class) lands in a public-facing doc, run a quick existence pass:

1. Steam search (titles, developer/publisher pages).
2. itch.io search.
3. USPTO TESS search in classes 9 and 41.
4. Generic web search.

Document any meaningful collisions in the project's `LORE.md` or
`DESIGN.md` for awareness.

## Reference taste vs. derivation

The slate openly references JRPG touchstones (Pokémon, Digimon, Fire
Emblem, Final Fantasy) for **mechanical and tonal grammar**, never
for IP. Specifically:

- **Allowed**: tactics-grid combat (FE), party-based ATB (FF),
  collectible creatures with branched evolution (Digimon-style),
  emotional companion bonds (FF X / Lost Odyssey), knowledge-gating
  across resets (Outer Wilds).
- **Not allowed**: any sprite, name, mechanic-name, or world-name
  that could be directly traced to a referenced franchise.
