# Cleave Studios — Brand

## Studio shape

- **Solo dev** indie studio.
- **Steam-first**, mobile ports later.
- **Premium one-time purchase + paid chapter expansions.** No gacha, no
  FOMO, no live-ops.
- **JRPG-rooted** taste: Pokémon, Digimon, Fire Emblem, Final Fantasy.
- **Pixel art** house style (32×32 / 48×48 sprites; see `ART-BIBLE.md`).
- **Original IP only.** No derivative names, taxonomies, or
  silhouettes (see `IP-GUIDELINES.md`).

## Why "Cleave"

"Cleave" is an auto-antonym — it means both *to split apart* and *to
cling tightly to*. That dual reading runs through all five titles on
the slate:

- **Ember & Echo** — emotions splitting from and clinging to the self.
- **Constellate** — discrete stars holding together as a single shape.
- **Chorus** — separate notes binding into a chord.
- **Grafted** — defeated foes severed and joined into new beasts.
- **The Lasting** — moments cleaved from the loop, and the loop
  holding fast.

One word, two readings, every game covered. **"Studios"** (rather than
"Games") differentiates from the Cleave Gaming Dota 2 esports team and
reads as a development house rather than a publisher of
cleave-mechanic content.

## Voice

Restrained, mythic, warm. We let the mechanic do the talking; we do
not over-promise. In copy: short sentences, concrete nouns, no hype
adjectives, no exclamation marks.

## Namespace check (April 2026)

No competing studio with the exact name "Cleave Studios" was found on
Steam, itch.io, the USPTO Trademark Search, or in indie-studio
directories. Adjacent risks to monitor:

- **Cleave Gaming** — Russian Dota 2 esports team. Different
  goods/services, so not a trademark blocker, but phonetically close.
  The "Studios" suffix mitigates direct confusion.
- **Game titles named *Cleave*** — Nintai Studios' boomer shooter
  (Steam app 3367620), GrizzlyBee Games' *Cleave Together*, Dark
  Cocooning's *Goblin Dice and Cleave*. Title collisions, not studio
  collisions; SEO will surface them before us.
- **Combat-mechanic noise** — "cleave" is a common ability term in
  WoW, Vermintide, Heroes of Newerth, Epic Seven. Search noise is
  significant but does not block use.
